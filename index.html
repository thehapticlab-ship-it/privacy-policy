Privacy Policy

Focus Planner: ADHD Friendly · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Focus Planner: ADHD Friendly (the "App"), a Wear OS application developed by The Haptic Lab ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • Your routines, tasks, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or health data.
  • We use Google Firebase to receive anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.
  • We do not sell your data or show ads.

Information stored on your device

The routines, tasks, timers, and preferences you create are stored locally on your watch. We have no access to this data. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may include this data in your personal Google backup, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information from your device.

Information collected automatically

The App uses the following Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the App crashes, it sends a crash report that may include device model, operating system version, app version, the time of the crash, and technical details of the error. We use it only to find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Analytics: collects anonymous usage information such as app opens, session duration, device type, OS version, approximate country, and an app-instance identifier. We use it to understand how the App is used and to improve it.

This data is processed by Google LLC. For details, see the Firebase Privacy and Security page and the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to alert you when a timer or task step finishes.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback and timer alerts.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep timers running accurately while the screen is off.

None of these permissions are used to collect personal information.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above (Google Firebase), or when required by law.

Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

Crash reports and analytics data collected through Firebase are not linked to your identity and are automatically deleted within 90 days. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, and analytics event data for up to 2 months. After that, only aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example, total number of sessions) are kept, and these cannot be used to identify you.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the App. Because the App has no accounts and the data we receive is not linked to your identity, we cannot locate individual users' data on request. Instead, all such data is deleted automatically within 90 days, as described above. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at thehapticlab@gmail.com.
